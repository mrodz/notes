## Brazil is taking good strides for environmental issues
- 1/3 of land is protected
- over 90% of brazilians feel like their country is doing work on climate change

## However...
- 2nd most dangerous country for land defenders in the world

1910-30's,  industrialization, urbanization, agriculture expand. In response, middle-class conservationists and intellectuals begin advocating for preservation of natural landscape through national park system, but policy implementation was haphazard.

## Environmental regression under Bolsonaro
- Rise in climate skeptical rhetoric
- Forest Code non-enforcement
- Amazon fires in 2019 contribute to tensions between Brazil and European donors
