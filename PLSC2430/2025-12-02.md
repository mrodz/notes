## Foreign Policy and Latin America

### Foreign Policy
- Strategies, policies, and objectives that shape how a state deals with other states and external organizations/actors
	- Trade
	- Aid
	- FDI/Portfolio investment
	- Conflict/war/security
	- Policy coordination over other issues

## Latin America as a "Periphery"

As a region, often discussed in terms of foreign powers extracting resources and dominating politically.
- European powers in the colonial era
- European and American creditors during the 19th century
- The US and Soviet Union during the Cold War
- The US and China today

Connection to dependency theory and World Systems Theory
- Larger, richer, more powerful states set the political and economic rules of the game at an international level.

## From Banana Republics to the Cold War
- USA highly involved in the internal affairs of Latin American countries from late 19th century-on
	- Intervenes to "impose order" in cases of political unrest or crisis
	- Conquers half of Mexico's territory, invades again during the Mexican Revolution
- Cold War creates new rationales for intervention
	- Truman Doctrine: U.S. should act to contain growth of authoritarianism
	- Cuban Revolution creates fear of growing soviet incursion in the hemisphere
	- Increasing aid, assistance, investments in soft power
	- Intervention in internal political conflicts to undermine "communist" threats (e.g. in Salvadorian and Nicaraguan civil wars)
	- Convert and overt efforts to support regime change (e.g. Bay of Bigs Invasion, Chile in 1973, Argentina in 1976)
