According to Transparency International, over half of Latin American citizens claim that corruption increased in their country over the last year

## Terms
- High-level corruption
	- Grand corruption by people who hold high-level office
- Petty Corruption
	- Small-level corruption by local officials and street-level bureaucrats

## Operation Car Wash and Petrobras/Odebrecht

Began as an investigation into low-level money laundering at gas stations, car washes, and groceries, but uncovered massive corruption scheme linked to state oil monopoly
- Petrobras overpaid for construction contracts and got kickbacks from the construction companies
- Funded re-election campaigns and private funds

Odebrecht
- Bribed politicians for high-profile construction contracts

## Mexico's Corrupt Governors
- 11 PRI governors serving under Peña Nieto in jail, placed under investigation, on the run
- Wide spread, uncontrollable corruption
	- Embezzlement of billions of dollars
	- Links to organized crime
	- Money laundering
	- Misappropriation of funds

## "Petty Corruption" in Latin America
- About 20% of LaTam ciitzens reported having to pay a bribe to an official over the last 12 months
	- Biggest offenders: police, public services, schools, hospitals
	- Cuts across gender, wealth, and other differences in society
- Evidence that corruption perception may even be worse
	- World Values Survey and other evidence suggests that majorities in most claim all or most servants/politicians are corrupt

## Corruption and State Weakness
- Corruption and institutional weakness feed into each other
- Low level corruption can enable high-level corruption and vice-versa

## Notebooks Scandal in Argentina
- Driver of a senior government official kept notebooks of his activities that were leaked to *La Nación*
- Driving to pick up and drop off suitcases full of bribe money from construction bosses to the houses of government officials

## Typologies of Corruption
- Corruption usually focuses on public office-holders, but can be extended to general institutions
- Corruption may be oriented toward personal wealth acquisition, but it is sometimes about political power/transactions more generally
- Arenas of corruption
	- Inputs: interest articulation and aggregation
	- Conversion: policy-making
	- Outputs: policy implementation and adjudication

## Administrative Corruption
- Policy implementers as well as policy makers are subject to corruption
- Exacerbated when those in charge of service delivery are poorly trained/paid
- Societies get trapped in a low equilibrium of bad service delivery
