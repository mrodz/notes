## The effect of a tax
- Tax creates wedge bedween/cost to the consumer and what sellers can keep
- A tax will increase price and reduce quantity
	- There will be later exceptions to this... it's not hard and fast
- A tax reduces the total social surplus
	- The social surplus is consumer surplus + producer surplus + tax revenue
- The reduction in surplus due to a policy is known as a deadweight loss.

## Solve quickly for equilibrium

$$
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
Q^D&=a-P \\
Q^S&=\frac{1}{b}P \\
\\
Q^D&=Q^S \\
a-P&=\frac{1}{b}P \\
&\text{...} \\
\end{split}
\end{equation}
$$

Effect of a tax imposed on seller on equilibrium price and quantity: algebra
$$
\begin{equation}
\begin{split}
P&=a-Q^D \\
P&=t+bQ^s \\
\\
a-Q^D&=t+bQ^s \\
Q &= \frac{a-t}{1+b} \\
\\
P_1^*&=a-Q_1^*\\
&=a-\big[\frac{a-t}{1+b}\big] \\
&=\frac{ab+t}{1+b}
\end{split}
\end{equation}
$$
