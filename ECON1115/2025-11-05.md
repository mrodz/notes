## Monopsony

- A monopsony is a market where there is only one buyer
- If a monopsonist wants to buy more, they need to consider their marginal expenditure: $$ME(Q)=P+\frac{\Delta P}{\Delta Q}Q$$
- How will they choose how much $Q$ to buy? They will choose $Q$ such that $$MWTP(Q)=ME(Q)$$
