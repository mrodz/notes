## Book Value
- Accounting value determined based on the cost of acquiring assets
- Almost always lower than market value and intrinsic value
- Changes every quarter with each 10Q
- Does not take into account the future of the business

## Intrinsic Value
- Value based on the expected financial performance of the business in the future
- Subjective value determined by financial analysts using a number of techniques
- Changes nay time there is new informaiton that might change the future performance of a business

## Market Value
- Value set by supply and demand in the open market


## Equity Dilution and the Treasury Stock Method
- Employees own stock options which give them the right to buy a piece of the companies' stock at a set price
