## Review

### Accounting Principles

1. Historical Cost Principle: Financial statements report companies' recourses at an initial historical cost
2. Revenue recognition principle: A company records revenue when it is earned and measurable
3. Matching principle: Costs associated with making a production or delivery of a service must be recorded in the same period as revenue generated from that product or service

### The financial statements

1. The Income Statement: Shows a company's revenue and costs over a period of time on an accrual basis
2. The Cash Flow Statement: Connects a company's accrual financial activities over a period of time to cash
3. The Balance Sheet: Outlines what a company owes and owns

Notes:
- The cash flow statement provides an aggregate view of the inflows and outflows of cash into a business during the stated period. It is divided into 3 sections
	- Cash flow from operations
	- Cash flow from investing (capital expenditures, asset sales/purchases)
	- Cash flow from financing (borrowing/pay-down of debt/stock repurchases/dividends)
