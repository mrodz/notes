## What are credit ratings?
- A signal of how trustworthy a company is
- What do Moody's, S&P, and Fitch look at?
	- The entity's payment history
	- The amount it currently owes and the types of debt it has
	- Current cash flows and income
	- The overall market or economic outlook

## How does a company issue debt?
They go to an investment bank! Banks come up with a solution including:
- Number of tranches
- Tenor - when it matures
- Tranche sizes
- Prediction of where their debt will price

Example: AAPL is taking out a $1B bond in two tranches: a $250mm 5yr, and a $750mm 10yr

## How does debt price?
- US treasuries are debt issued by the US government
- US treasuries help set interest rates for US debt.
	- US 10yr treasury: 3.96% yield
- Companies issue debt using US treasuries as an "anchor"

## How do you figure out where new debt will price?
- See where their old 10yr debt priced
- See where competitors' 10yrs are pricing
- Adjustments:
	- Tenor
		- If debt has longer tenor than comps, add bps
	- New issue concession
		- We have to throw investors a few bps of new issue concession (NIC) so that they buy new debt instead of old debt

When the 10yr increases, spread falls.
